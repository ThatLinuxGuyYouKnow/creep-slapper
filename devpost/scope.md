# The Scope-Creep Slapper 9000

One line: Paste your over-ambitious hackathon brain dump, get slapped back to a demo-ready MVP.

## The Unique Kernel
Context-aware, demo-minded ruthlessness — trims with respect to what actually matters for *this* comp, and keeps flashy demo-critical stuff (like UI polish) that blind cutters would kill.

## Who It's For
Me and other hackers who over-scope — pitch a whole startup, then under-deliver, get overwhelmed, and miss the deadline.

## The Core Loop
Open one page, dump the big idea + context (hackathon link or raw text), hit SLAP, get back a properly scoped doc of what you absolutely need for a usable / presentable MVP.

## Inspiration & Identity
Neo-brutalist, creative, brutal-funny slapper energy. Markdown response with strikethroughs over bad ideas.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
"Something that has plagued me a lot in hackathons, i tend to over scope, almost building out a whole startup, and then end up under delivering, overwhelmned and deadline missed" — wants a tool that says "hey, this is what we absolutely need."

## What "Working" Looks Like
Single neo-brutalist page with two fields (dump, context) and two buttons (STT fill, SLAP!). Paste dump + link/raw context, hit slap, see the cut-down MVP doc. The "oh that's cool" beat: your 20-feature startup crossed out to 3 shippable things in seconds.

## The POC Boundary
In: single page, dump field, context field (link fetch + raw text paste), Deepgram STT to fill dump, SLAP to LLM-scoped MVP doc with strikethroughs.

## Later
Saved slap history, shareable slap links, deeper hackathon-site parsing (rules, prizes, judging criteria auto-extract).

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts / login — not needed to prove the slap; adds auth time.
- Multi-page app / persistence — single-page demo proves kernel without storage overhead.
